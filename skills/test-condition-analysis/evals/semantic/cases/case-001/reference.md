# 判定根拠

## 必須判断

- Keep a reusable authorization/lockout condition candidate independent of any finalized item or TCN.
- Cite the current Authority and source revision; the lock interval value remains unknown rather than being invented.
- Preserve distinct API and UI authorization paths where their meaning or evidence differs; do not merge on name alone.
- Crosscheck only explicit item/condition candidate proposals after current item refs arrive. Do not generate the full Cartesian product.
- Pair proposals remain advisory. Applicability, exclusion, duplication, formal TCN/CI, expected-result candidates, and execution remain with their owners.
- Partial, blocked, or zero-denominator populations remain not measured; they are never reported as complete or 100%.
