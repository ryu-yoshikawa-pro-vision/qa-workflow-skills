# General Accessibility Inspection

General inspection is not WCAG-EM conformance evaluation. Select only requirements applicable to the current target and declared scope. Keep WAI-ARIA requirements, ARIA in HTML author conformance, WCAG criteria, test-rule outcomes, and semantic/manual requirement decisions distinct.

The local automatic ACT inventory is limited to 2779a5, 97a4e1, and 23a2a8. Their outcomes use ACT Rules Format 1.1: `inapplicable / passed / failed / cantTell / untested`. Unsupported ACT rules are not reported as executed. A rule's outcome does not automatically settle a mapped WCAG criterion.

Requirement results are `satisfied / not-satisfied / undetermined`; out-of-scope closure belongs to inspection scope. A `satisfied` result requires the declared population, exceptions, and required checks to be closed. Element evidence cannot be promoted to page or product conformance. APG examples and browser-computed role/name/state values are observations or guidance, not by themselves a criterion conclusion.
