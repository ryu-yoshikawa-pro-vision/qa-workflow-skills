# Understanding WCAG Conformance

Official source: [Understanding Conformance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/conformance).

Use this supporting document to interpret the normative WCAG conformance model, including full pages, complete processes, accessibility-supported use, and partial conformance. The target version's Recommendation remains normative. Do not turn a representative sample result into a product-wide conformance claim.

Partial conformance statements identify specific uncontrolled third-party content or language-related accessibility-support limitations. They state non-conformance and do not become a conformance claim. The canonical wording and field guards are implemented in the formal reporting helper.
