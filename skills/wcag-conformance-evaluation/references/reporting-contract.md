# Reporting Contract

Step 5.1 report closure tracks each Step 1–4 outcome, every not-satisfied conformance requirement and Success Criterion example, any requested all-occurrence coverage, and accessible report output. Do not collapse results into an aggregate score. Derive a non-empty `required_steps` list from current applicability decisions; each listed step must be `complete`, while `not-applicable` is only for a step excluded by those decisions. At final closure, provide the full current output of `materialize_plan()` as `canonical_criterion_plan`; `close-report` re-materializes it from its recorded sample, variation, target, process, evaluation, and revision inputs. It derives the required criterion refs from that plan and checks each result against the plan's evaluation revision, sample, variation, criterion, and process identity. The caller-declared ref set is only a consistency check and cannot reduce the plan's result set. Partial execution remains allowed before final closure.

When Step 4.1 or Step 4.2 is required, their current result scope must match the canonical criterion plan; a completed Step 4.1 or 4.2 outcome also requires results even if the caller omitted the step from `required_steps`. A completed Step 3.1, 3.2, or 3.3 selection likewise means an evaluated sample/process set exists and cannot be closed with an empty result scope. Thus Step 4.2 being `not-applicable` does not waive Step 4.1 results. `close-report` blocks missing/extra results and any `undetermined`, stale, unknown, duplicate, or mismatched result. When Step 4.1 is legitimately `not-applicable` and Step 4.2 is required, provide the current results for the complete processes.

Plan self-consistency is not proof that its basis came from the current approved evaluation. Formal completion therefore goes through `qa-workflow`: it reads the current evaluation record from the workflow owner's SQLite provider, compares evaluation ref/revision, WCAG version/level, scope evidence, selected sample/variation/process sets, canonical Plan and current result set, then conditionally stores report completion at the read provider revision and rereads it. The production provider is limited to one host with a local filesystem at the existing `qa.workflow_state_root`; unavailable or unsupported storage remains blocked. The WCAG `close-report` runtime result is an unresolved persistence candidate until that owner operation succeeds. A stored record does not itself establish user approval; preserve existing evaluation inputs, evidence and authority refs.

The Random Sample section preserves the target and actual counts, selection method, exact selected sample refs from the machine selector, selection status, and exhaustion or blocked reason. `target_count` must equal `random_target_count()` for the current structured sample set; exhaustion and incomplete candidate inventory remain distinct outcomes. When no sample was selected, render `None selected` and retain the helper's closure status and evidence.

Step 5.2 Evaluation Specifics is optional and stores safe archive/evidence refs, path, settings/actions, tool/browser/AT versions, and method at the correct evaluation/sample/check scope. Do not store passwords, tokens, cookies, storage state, or unnecessary personal data.

Step 5.3 Evaluation Statement is WCAG 2.2 only. Full requires every nonoptional methodology requirement, every selected sample at target level, and owner commitment. Generation consumes a complete report closure and current conformance results with the same evaluation ref/revision, target, and exact criterion-ref set; the boolean inputs alone do not establish completion or conformance. Partial additionally requires current nonconforming results and every nonconforming area to be explained by third-party content or lack of accessibility support for languages. WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 reports do not include this Evaluation Statement.

A WCAG Conformance Claim requires full-scope claim evidence and required fields; representative samples alone are insufficient. For `all-pages-evaluated`, a `scope_expression` must be backed by an exact complete URI inventory before a claim is generated. A non-enumerable scope may use the existing `assurance-process` method only when its complete-process evidence refs are included in the evidence set and all covered-page criterion and requirement results are current and complete. A population-complete boolean alone is not proof. The claim URI comes from the target-version catalog. A third-party monitoring/repair full claim additionally requires all affected pages to be identifiable, monitoring capability, and repair within two business days. A Statement of Partial Conformance is a separate type for third-party content or language and follows the canonical wording in the report contract.

Step 5.5 EARL JSON-LD is generated only when requested. Every result must identify the supplied current evaluation ref and revision and have `freshness_status: current` before it can produce a current `earl:passed` or `earl:failed`; stale or mismatched results are rejected. Explicit current `not-run` results may still map to `earl:untested`. Use stable evaluation/result IRIs, canonical Success Criterion URI, fixed graph shape and byte serialization, and the fixed outcome mapping. A complete criterion with no applicable population maps to `earl:inapplicable`; applicable `satisfied` maps to `earl:passed`. No other output format renderer is added.
